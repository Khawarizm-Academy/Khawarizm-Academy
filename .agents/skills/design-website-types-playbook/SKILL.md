# Website Types Playbook

Pick the type, copy its page inventory, then apply the shared skills ([`design-frontend-execution-playbook`](../design-frontend-execution-playbook/SKILL.md)). Stores: [`design-ecommerce`](../design-ecommerce/SKILL.md); restaurants: [`design-restaurants-and-food`](../design-restaurants-and-food/SKILL.md); campaign pages: [`design-landing-pages-and-conversion`](../design-landing-pages-and-conversion/SKILL.md); app dashboards: [`design-dashboards-and-data-viz`](../design-dashboards-and-data-viz/SKILL.md).

## Quick matrix

| Type | Primary goal | Core pages | Tone | Key conversion |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Corporate / services | trust -> inquiry | Home, Services (+detail), About, Case studies, Contact | confident, clear | contact/quote form, call |
| Portfolio / personal brand | credibility -> hire | Home, Work (+case study), About, Contact | personal, crafted | contact/booking |
| Blog / magazine | readership, authority | Home, Category, Article, Author, Search | editorial, readable | newsletter, return visits |
| SaaS marketing | sign-ups / demo | Home, Features, Pricing, Integrations, Docs/Blog, Login | crisp, product-led | free trial / demo |
| Education / courses | enrollment | Home, Catalog, Course detail, Instructor, Pricing, FAQ, Student dashboard | encouraging, structured | enroll / book trial |
| Nonprofit | donations, volunteers | Home, Mission, Programs, Impact, Donate, Volunteer, Reports | warm, honest | donate |
| Real estate | listing inquiries | Home, Search/Listings, Listing detail, Agents, Areas, Contact | aspirational, trustworthy | inquiry/viewing request |
| Clinic / health | appointments | Home, Services, Doctors, Booking, Insurance, Locations, Articles | calm, credible | book appointment/call |
| Events | registrations | Home, Agenda, Speakers, Venue, Tickets, Sponsors, FAQ | energetic | buy ticket/register |
| Directory / marketplace | matches, listings | Home/search, Listing, Category, Profile, Add listing | utilitarian, trusted | contact/lead, listing |

## Corporate / services
Hero stating who you help and the outcome; proof (logos, numbers, testimonials); services as outcome-focused cards each linking to a detail page; process; case studies with measurable results; team/about for trust; clear contact with several channels. Avoid vague jargon and stock handshake photos.

## Portfolio / personal brand
Lead with your **best 3-6 projects**, each a case study: problem, role, process, result, visuals. Distinct personality (type, layout, motion) but fast and accessible; easy contact; resume download optional. Do not hide work behind heavy animation or require a click to see anything.

## Blog / magazine
Readability first: 65ch measure, large type, generous line-height, strong hierarchy, table of contents for long posts, author boxes, related posts, categories/tags, search, newsletter capture, share links, reading time. SEO: article structured data, clean URLs, internal links, fast pages ([`seo-on-page-content`](../seo-on-page-content/SKILL.md)). Pagination or load-more with crawlable links.

## SaaS marketing
Hero with product screenshot/demo, benefits over features, use cases by persona, integrations, security/compliance, **transparent pricing** with a recommended plan and a comparison table, docs and changelog, status page link. Primary CTA "Start free"; secondary "Book a demo".

## Education and course platforms
Catalog with filters (level, topic, language, price), course cards (instructor, duration, level, rating), detail page (outcomes, curriculum, prerequisites, instructor, reviews, FAQ, sticky enroll CTA), a learner dashboard (continue learning, progress, certificates, live sessions), exams/competitions where relevant, parent/teacher info for youth audiences, clear safety and privacy for minors. Accessible, mobile-first, bilingual/RTL when needed.

## Nonprofit
Mission in one sentence, **impact numbers and stories**, programs, transparent finances/reports, a frictionless donation flow (preset amounts, recurring option, local payment methods, receipts), volunteer sign-up, trust badges. Emotional but honest imagery with consent.

## Real estate
Search-first: map + list, filters (price, rooms, area, type), saved searches, listing cards with the key facts, detail pages with gallery, floor plan, map, neighborhood info, agent CTA, mortgage calculator; fast image galleries; structured data for listings; lead forms with phone/WhatsApp.

## Clinic / health
Reassurance and clarity: services, doctors with credentials, how booking works, insurance, locations and hours, emergency info, patient privacy; online booking with confirmation; accessible, high-contrast, readable; careful claims (no unverified medical promises); health-related content reviewed by professionals.

## Events
Date/venue above the fold, countdown (honest), agenda with filters, speakers, ticket tiers and clear checkout, venue map and travel info, sponsors, FAQ, post-event recap; mobile ticket delivery.

## Directory / marketplace
Search + filters + map, consistent listing cards, trust (verification, reviews), contact flows, listing creation wizard with moderation, spam control, SEO-friendly category and profile pages with structured data.

## Shared requirements for every type

- Information architecture and navigation: [`design-ux-patterns-and-microcopy`](../design-ux-patterns-and-microcopy/SKILL.md).
- Responsive, accessible, fast: [`fe-responsive-layout-and-fixes`](../fe-responsive-layout-and-fixes/SKILL.md), [`fe-accessibility`](../fe-accessibility/SKILL.md), [`perf-frontend-web-vitals`](../perf-frontend-web-vitals/SKILL.md).
- SEO foundation: [`seo-technical`](../seo-technical/SKILL.md), [`seo-on-page-content`](../seo-on-page-content/SKILL.md), [`seo-structured-data`](../seo-structured-data/SKILL.md).
- Legal basics: privacy policy, terms, cookie consent where required, accessibility statement for regulated markets.

## Checklist

- [ ] Type chosen; page inventory and primary conversion defined.
- [ ] Tone and imagery match the audience; proof appears near the CTA.
- [ ] Shared requirements (IA, a11y, speed, SEO, legal) covered.
